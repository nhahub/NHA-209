\# Inventory Management Analytics



\##  Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing inventory performance, monitoring purchasing and replenishment activities, and evaluating overall supply chain efficiency. The goal is to provide clear, data-driven insights that support better decision-making and ensure optimal stock levels across the supply chain.



\## Objectives

\- Analyze inventory performance and stock behavior.

\- Monitor purchasing and replenishment cycles.

\- Measure operational efficiency and financial performance.

\- Evaluate product availability and service levels.

\- Track accuracy, forecasting quality, and stock optimization metrics.



\##  Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)



\### 1️⃣ Efficiency KPIs

\- Inventory Turnover Ratio  

\- sell through rate

\- Days in inventory

\- Stock Movement Efficiency  



\### 2️⃣ Availability \& Service KPIs

\- Stock Availability  

\- Replenishment Efficiency  

\- Lead Time Efficiency  - Fill Rate  

\- Backorder Rate  



\### 3️⃣ Accuracy \& Forecast KPIs

\- Forecast Accuracy  

\- Demand Planning Accuracy  

\- Inventory Accuracy 

\### 4️⃣ Operational KPIs

\- perfect order rate

\- Supplier Lead Time  

\- Purchase Order Cycle Time  

\- Back order rate



\### 5️⃣ Financial KPIs

\- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)  

\- Gross Margin Return on Investment (GMROI)  

\- Stock Value  



\## 🛠 Tools \& Technologies

\- \*\*Power BI\*\* – For dashboarding, interactive insights, and DAX measures  

\- \*\*SQL\*\* – For data extraction, cleaning, transformation, and modeling  

\- \*\*DAX\*\* – For calculating KPIs and business logic within Power BI  



\## 📂 Project Deliverables

\- Complete Power BI dashboard for inventory and purchase analytics  

\- Data model integrating inventory, sales, and purchasing tables  

\- KPI-driven insights across operational, financial, and service dimensions
